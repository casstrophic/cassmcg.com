I'm Cass (she/her) and I live near Edinburgh in Scotland.

I write mostly shortform fiction and poetry as well as tabletop game material. At the moment much of it is for my own satisfaction and usually shown to a small audience of close friends, but I published some here.

My passion for music has led me to pick up the bass guitar and hardware synthesisers. I am not good at playing them yet, but I am having a lot of fun.

I am somewhat decent at photography and enjoy it very much. The good stuff goes on Flickr. I find myself right at home doing architecture and macro, but I am very interested in portraiture.

My academic background is in linguistics. My main interests are historical linguistics and study of writing as a language-augmenting technology; my dissertation was a comparison and investigation of Cherokee, Korean and Japanese syllabaries. I remain very fond of constructed languages and linguistic reconstruction, my initial gateways into linguistics.

Nowadays I work on software. I have meandered from video handling/streaming to web frontends to large dataset processing. I mostly work in Python, but I have past experience with TypeScript, PHP and Kotlin.
