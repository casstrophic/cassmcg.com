Today I Live

with Cristina Delgado

  • In the grass, unmoving but for a slowly swaying tail.
    In the wind, gliding with wings still, gaze pinned on you.
    In the waters, circling with the economy of a shark.
  • I want to devour you every dawn.
    Would this just rob me of an even sweeter feast?
    For now, stillness and enduring.
  • I will sink
     my teeth in your flesh,
    split your ribcage,
     drink your blood,
      savour your heart.
  • I dream you will taste
     of fruit and wine,
    you will be sweet
     and bitter,
      and overwhelming.
  • I think it will kill me.
  • Eyes closed, I stay myself in the curve
     between your neck and your shoulder.
  • A gasp away from the collarbone,
     I slowly breathe you in.
  • There is salt and there is sweat,
     and many notes I can’t resolve.
  • Blurred stripes flash
     Wings pull in for a dive
      Whipping fin changes course.
